Stuff about Tamarin Monkeys

Emperor tamarin monkeys are small primates with long, white mustaches and bright orange fur that make them easy to recognize in the rainforest canopy. They are highly social animals that often travel in groups and communicate with soft calls and alert body movements.

These curious monkeys spend much of their day foraging for fruit, insects, and seeds while moving carefully through the treetops.
